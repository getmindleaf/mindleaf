When you're overwhelmed, your nervous system is stuck in overdrive. Your thoughts are racing, your chest feels tight, and nothing feels manageable.

Box breathing is the pattern specifically matched to this state. Here's exactly how it works.

## What is Box Breathing?

Box breathing is a 4-4-4-4 pattern — inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4, exhale for 4, hold for 4. One full box takes 16 seconds.

## Why It Works for Overwhelm

The equal rhythm of box breathing gives your nervous system something predictable to hold onto when everything else feels chaotic. The holds create brief pauses that interrupt the racing thought cycle.

## How to Do It

1. Inhale slowly through your nose for 4 seconds
2. Hold your breath for 4 seconds
3. Exhale slowly through your mouth for 4 seconds
4. Hold for 4 seconds
5. Repeat 3 to 5 times

## When to Use It

- When your to-do list feels impossible
- Before a difficult conversation
- When your mind won't stop racing at night
- Any moment where everything feels like too much at once

One minute of box breathing — roughly 4 repetitions — is enough to shift your nervous system out of overdrive.
