Common questions

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → find Mindleaf → tap Cancel Subscription. Your Pro access continues until the end of the current billing period.

I was charged but the app still shows Free.

Open the app → go to the upgrade screen → tap Restore purchases. If that doesn't work, email us at support@getmindleaf.com with your Apple ID email and we'll sort it out.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: tap the person icon → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, all session history, and cancels any active subscription. It cannot be undone.

I'm not receiving the verification email.

Check your spam or junk folder — the email comes from noreply@getmindleaf.com. If it's not there, tap Resend on the verification screen. Codes expire after 10 minutes. If you're still having trouble, email us at support@getmindleaf.com.

Can I use Mindleaf without an account?

Yes. Mindleaf works fully as a guest — no account required. Your session history is saved on your device. If you reinstall the app, your guest history will be lost. Create a free account to keep your history safe across reinstalls.

Does Mindleaf work offline?

The breathing sessions work offline. The ambient soundscapes require an internet connection to stream. Account sync and session history saving also require a connection.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com → sign in → find the Mindleaf charge → Request a refund. Apple typically processes refund requests within a few days.
